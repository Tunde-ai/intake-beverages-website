InTake Paris — Apricot Mist

Eyes of Paris, La Ville aux Mille Regards (The City of a Thousand Gazes)

Dear Parisians, What better way to pay homage to the City of Love than a beverage that reflects the most magical time of the day — golden hour. This beverage carries you through a love story where Dusk meets Dawn.

Elderflower, sourced from Brittany's woodlands — a wild beauty, historically cherished for aromatic oils and remedies, known for its subtle, elegant character appreciated by a gentleman's palate.

Apricot from Roussillon valleys represents southern warmth, bringing brightness to Rhône Valley summers. Nostalgic and quietly luxurious — very southern-French in spirit.

When they met, it was connection at first sight. All of the Eyes of Paris lit, glowing as they lay watch, each seeing in the other what they themselves could not. The sun shed tears of joy in the form of dew, pouring from the blades of leaves, watering the taste of Apricot Mist.
