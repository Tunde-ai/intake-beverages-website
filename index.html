INTAKE PARIS - APRICOT MIST

Eyes of Paris, La Ville aux Mille Regards (The City of a Thousand Gazes)

Dear Parisians,

What better way to pay homage to the City of Love than a beverage that reflects the most magical time of the day—golden hour. This beverage carries you through a love story where Dusk meets Dawn.

Sourced from the woodlands of Brittany lies the wild beauty—elderflower (Dower). Long used for aromatic oils and ancient remedies, French elderflower holds a natural elegance: subtle and seasonal... almost understated. Its modest notes are the kind a gentleman's palate would savor.

Hailing from the valleys of Roussillon lies southern warmth—apricot (Dual). Often associated with stone-washed villages, French abricot brings brightness and charm to Rhône Valley summers. Apricot is nostalgic and quietly luxurious— very southern-French in spirit. Its warm notes are the kind a lady's palate would adore.

As a summer evening approached through the day, that elusive joy overcame the night. When they met, it was connection at first sight. All of the Eyes of Paris lit, glowing as they lay watch, each seeing in the other what they themselves could not. The sun shed tears of joy in the form of dew, pouring from the blades of leaves, watering the taste of Apricot Mist.

With Love,

...La Ville Lumière

OVERVIEW

InTake Global & Beverages is a Florida-based, independently owned and operated company founded in 2019 by creative pharmaceutical Xavier R Artis. Committed to social causes, the company is guided by its motto, “InTake is not just products, it's the promise of progress,” focusing on education, wellness, and raising awareness. Its flagship products, InTake Energy + Water drinks, along with custom and limited-edition white label offerings, are designed to support and promote diversity within the beverage industry globally.
